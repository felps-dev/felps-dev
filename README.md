## 🧭 About me

Engineer, founder, builder. I currently lead engineering at [**Numa**](https://numa.com) — a Silicon Valley startup shipping AI-powered automation for the auto industry. Before Numa I co-founded **two Brazilian tech companies** and spent the better part of a decade learning what it actually takes to put software in front of people who depend on it.

I care about three things: **shipping**, **architecture that gets out of the way**, and **mentoring teams to ship faster**.
